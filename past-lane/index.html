Every lap you drive comes back at you, head-on. Dodge your own past.

How to play

Your car accelerates on its own. After each lap, a ghost of that lap drives the circuit in reverse, on the exact line you took.

Hit a ghost and you lose a shield. Lose all three and the run is over. Skim past one for a near miss: bonus points, a combo, and nitro.

← → or A D steer · ↓/S brake · Space/↑ nitro · P pause · R restart · M mute

Use the on-screen buttons to steer, brake and fire nitro.

Tip: the line you drive now is the line you must avoid later.

Questions

Is the quote really fixed?

Yes. If we find something the quote did not cover, we stop, show you, and write a new quote before doing more.

Which areas do you cover?

Edmonton, plus St. Albert and Sherwood Park. Add your postal code to the form and we confirm it in our reply.

Do you replace furnaces as well as repair them?

Yes. When a repair costs more than half of a new unit, the quote shows both so you can choose.

How do I pay?

Debit, credit card or e-transfer once the job is done and you are happy with it.
